# Surface: Game Night Scores app (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page app (Scores, Rounds, Timer, Players, History, round sheet, New game, winner card). Mode: Operate.
Audience: the owner keeping score one-handed on a phone at family game night; bright kitchen tables and dim living rooms (both themes required).
Constraints: keep layout, flow and the teal brand colour (user, 2026-11-10). Avoid childish, busy, flashy, or serious.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is the family's score pad, printed in teal and filled in pencil. It refuses the stock card-list scoreboard (rounded white cards on grey, pill tabs).
OWN-WORLD: Printed teal header band and teal form rules; white pad ground (light) / deep green-black pad (dark); Oswald small-caps column heads; Archivo semi-condensed tabular numerals in graphite; negatives in red pencil; player marks as outlined initial boxes in the four tile colours; underline tabs; rows separated by rules, not cards.
STORY: The scorekeeper sees who leads instantly, enters a round in seconds, and the pad remembers every night.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Teal header band: game name left, New game right. Example notice (dashed). Underline tabs. Column heads (#, Player · N rounds, Total) over a teal rule; ranked rows: rank, initial box, name with "N behind", graphite total right. Pinned "+ Score a round" at bottom.
